**Subject: SoPo Air Quality Community Assembly 10/15**

Hello fellow South Portland Community Members, 

You’re invited to a **community assembly on Thursday, Oct. 15 in South Portland** to discuss the harmful air quality near tank farms in South Portland and to prepare for the related City Council workshop on Oct. 27\. 

**Please [RSVP here](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeTd9xvZDCZ7zeoINrAy39P27RTjnXYKAYCENgUpnYdWkyQ-g/viewform)** (RSVP not required, but helpful for planning\!)

**When: Thursday, October 15th, 5:30-7 p.m.**

* Snacks and community-building 5:30-6 p.m.  
* Program from 6-7p.m. 

**Where:** Guptill Hall, First Congregational Church UCC of South Portland, 301 Cottage Rd; use Mitchell Road or Sawyer parking lot entrances.

**What:** A Community Assembly to 1.) learn about the recent history of corporations polluting our air, 2.) what we want the City Council to do about it, and 3.) what actions we can take individually and together to make our community safer.

**Why now? This is a critical moment for our community to show up in force and ask the city to take action.** There is a city council workshop on Tuesday Oct. 27 at 6:30 p.m. at South Portland High School. The council will hear from the state Department of Environmental Protection about data that found high readings of the carcinogen benzene, exceeding the state air quality standard, near tanks. (See attached one-page explainer and read [this article](https://insideclimatenews.org/news/11032026/south-portland-maine-petroleum-storage-tanks-emissions/) for more information)

**Who:** This event will be facilitated by a group of concerned community members, and hosted in collaboration with Protect South Portland. 

Thank you, and please spread the word\!
