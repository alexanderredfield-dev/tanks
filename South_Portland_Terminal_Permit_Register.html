Glossary

License and permit types

TermMeaning
Ch. 600 marine oil terminal license O-######-91-x-RDEP license to operate a marine oil terminal, under 38 M.R.S. §545 and 06-096 CMR ch. 600. Term is at least 12 months and no more than 60 months.
Part 70 / Title V air license A-###-70-x-RFederal Clean Air Act operating permit for major sources, issued by Maine DEP under 06-096 CMR ch. 140. Five-year term. Renewal application due 6 to 18 months before expiration.
Ch. 115 air license A-###-71-x-xAir emission license for minor sources under 06-096 CMR ch. 115. Ten-year term on the licenses reviewed.
NSR license A-###-77-x-MNew Source Review license for building or changing emissions equipment. Appears as "77" in license numbers.
MEPDES permit ME#######Maine Pollutant Discharge Elimination System permit. Maine's delegated Clean Water Act permit for discharges to surface water. Five-year term.
WDL W######-5S-x-RWaste Discharge License under 38 M.R.S. §413, issued alongside each MEPDES permit. The "5S" code is the fuel oil terminal/transfer category in DEP's license list.
MSGP / MER numberMulti-Sector General Permit for industrial stormwater. Transfer orders list the facility's MER number.
Hydrostatic test waterWater used to pressure-test tanks and piping. Discharged under the MEPDES permit.
Chapter 171Maine rule for petroleum storage facilities. Requires fenceline benzene, toluene, ethylbenzene, and xylene monitoring (EPA Methods 325A/B).
Chapter 170Maine rule on degassing petroleum storage tanks and vessels.
Chapter 112Maine rule for bulk gasoline terminals with 20,000 gallons per day or more of throughput.
City flammable-liquids licenseSouth Portland annual license under Ch. 14, §14-32. Expires April 1 each year.
Site-plan approvalCity planning approval for a facility's layout and use. Can lapse if not acted on.
Fire Marshal tank permitState Fire Marshal permit for above-ground oil tanks over 60 gallons.

Terms used in the registers

TermMeaning
Administrative continuationIf a complete renewal is filed before expiration, the existing permit stays in effect until DEP acts on it.
(calc.)Date computed from a stated term. Not printed in the document.
Basis: Stated / InferredStated means the date is printed in the permit. Inferred means it is assumed from a typical term.
File-byDeadline or window to file a renewal. The 180-day MEPDES figure reflects typical practice and is not yet confirmed from rule text.
Major / minor sourceMajor sources have potential emissions above Title V thresholds (for example, 50 tons per year of VOC). Minor sources are below.
PTE (potential to emit)Maximum emissions if a facility ran continuously at full capacity. Determines major or minor status.
tpyTons per year.
VOCVolatile organic compounds.
BPT (Best Practical Treatment)DEP's technology standard for controlling emissions from existing sources.
License letter codesLetters after the number track each action. Observed suffixes: R = renewal, T = transfer, M = minor revision, A = amendment. Earlier letters are the sequence of prior actions.
